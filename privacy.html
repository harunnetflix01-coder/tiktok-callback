Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what data Clip Borsası (app name "kickshorts", the "Service") handles.

1. Data we receive from TikTok

When the Clip Borsası team connects its own TikTok account, TikTok gives the Service an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID, the granted scopes, and basic creator information (username, nickname, allowed privacy levels, maximum video length). We use the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish only.

2. How we use it

This data is used for one purpose: to post videos that a person on our team has reviewed and approved to our own TikTok account, and to check whether a post succeeded. We do not read other users' data, and we do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored in a private file on the operator's own computer and are never published or shared. They are refreshed automatically and can be revoked at any time from TikTok's "Manage app permissions" page.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share this data with anyone. Videos are sent only to the platforms we post to.

5. Visitors of this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; see GitHub's privacy statement for the hosting provider's logs.

6. Deletion

Disconnecting the app on TikTok revokes access; we then delete the stored tokens. Creators can ask us to remove any clip.

7. Contact

Open an issue at github.com/harunnetflix01-coder/tiktok-callback/issues.
